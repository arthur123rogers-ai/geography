Captain's playbook

Powerplay = warm-up chores

Open with easy wins (beds, cushions, dishes) to build runs fast and avoid early wickets.

Middle overs = combos

Pair related chores: vacuum + mop, laundry wash + hang. Keep the batter whose specialty matches the combo.

Death overs = high effort

Finish with tough tasks (fridge clean, bathroom scrub). Save your highest skill finisher to avoid collapse.
